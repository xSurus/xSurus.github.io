I’m an MSc Computer Science student at ETH Zurich on the machine learning track, graduating in February 2027.

This summer I was an SRE intern at Google. On my 20% project I designed a forget-gated attention variant and pre-trained small decoder-only LMs in JAX on 16 A100s, improving long-context retrieval by 10% over softmax attention. Before that I shipped ML models to production at tabular, built an LLM product for a biopharma client at BCG X, and worked as a software engineer at two startups.

Right now I contribute to Hugging Face’s LeRobot and work on robot learning.
